# Footnotes

A sentence with a footnote[^long] and another[^short].

[^long]: The footnote's first paragraph, long enough that a formatter wrapping
    at forty columns has to break it somewhere.

    Its second paragraph, indented under the definition, long enough to wrap as
    well.

[^short]: A short one.

After the footnotes, a paragraph long enough that the formatter wraps it.
